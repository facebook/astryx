fix: extend URL scheme sanitization to autolinks and reference links

The initial fix only covered inline `[text](url)` links and `![alt](src)`
images. Two additional XSS vectors remained:

1. **Angle-bracket autolinks**: `<javascript:alert(1)>` matched the
   `ANGLE_SCHEME` regex (`[a-zA-Z][a-zA-Z0-9+.-]*`) and produced a
   clickable link node with a `javascript:` href.

2. **Reference links**: A link definition `[label]: javascript:alert(1)`
   followed by `[label]` also produced a dangerous link node.

Both paths now filter through the same `isSafeUrl()` helper that rejects
`javascript:`, `vbscript:`, and `data:text/html` schemes.

Added test for angle-bracket autolink XSS rejection.
