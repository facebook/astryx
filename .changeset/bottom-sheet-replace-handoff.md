[feat] `BottomSheet` replaces a sheet that closes in the same update another opens. When the top sheet of a stack closes and exactly one other sheet opens in the same update (one state value naming the open step does this), the new sheet replaces it instead of stacking on it: it rises above the replaced sheet, a taller replaced sheet moves down to meet its top edge at the same time, the replaced sheet fades only after both motions finish and is inert and hidden from assistive technology from the start, the scrim holds steady with no dip, focus moves into the new sheet, and closing the flow later returns focus to the element that opened its first sheet. This is the handoff `BottomSheetSwitcher` runs between its steps, now available to standalone sheets; a multi-step flow can be written as one `BottomSheet` per step with no switcher. A covered sheet closing while a sheet opens, several sheets changing at once, or a close and an open in different updates keep the ordinary push and pop motion, and existing stacking behaviour is unchanged. A replace is recognised only when the move was made from inside the closing sheet (the focused control was in it, as a step's own Continue or Back button is); a close and an open driven from a control outside it, by two unrelated owners, stay a pop and a push with their own focus return. Non-modal sheets (`hasScrim={false}`) neither stack nor replace. The private stack registry now keeps one state per page (shared by every copy of the package on it, guarded by shape) and one stack per document. No prop, type, or export changes; a sheet on its own behaves exactly as before.
