[feat] ChatLayout keeps its composer on top of the software keyboard. On a phone the dock rises and falls with the keyboard, in step with it on the keyboard's own spring, instead of staying underneath it until the browser scrolls the whole page; a conversation at its newest message stays there, and the rest of the transcript can still be scrolled above the composer. A tap on the composer's text focuses it without the page scrolling. Nothing changes where no software keyboard appears.
