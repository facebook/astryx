[feat] Inside a `ContextMenu`, a `DropdownMenuSubMenu` with `header` or `footer` shows them as it does in a `DropdownMenu`: its flyout, or its drilled-in view on a phone, is a modal dialog beside the menu holding the header, the rows and the footer, and the bottom sheet shows a data item's own regions above and below that level's actions. Before, a `ContextMenu` showed the sub-menu's rows alone.
