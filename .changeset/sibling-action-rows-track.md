[feat] Toolbar, Banner and Toast: a press on one of a row's buttons can slide across the others, the highlight follows the pointer, and lifting acts on the button under it (or on nothing outside the row), with a finger, a pen or a mouse. Toolbar skips the fields it holds, and a Banner or Toast row tracks only with two or more buttons. Under a coarse pointer each button's hit area reaches 44 px with no visual change. Keyboard and screen-reader activation are unchanged.
