[feat] SegmentedControl: under a finger, on every touch screen, the control tracks the touch. Holding the selected segment lifts it, casting the theme's high elevation shadow; the lift's size follows the theme's spacing. Dragging carries the selection along with the finger, and the segment nearest the finger is chosen when it lets go (`onChange` fires once, at the lift). While the lifted selection is dragged, the selected label's colour follows it, with a margin around each boundary so a finger resting on one never flickers; label weights change only at the lift, so nothing reflows under the finger. A tap selects through the browser's own click. A tap on another segment dims it while the finger is down, then slides the selection there, lifted for the first part of the way; a theme sets that dim with `opacity` on the `segmented-control-item` target's `pressed` state (0.2 by default). A vertical drag that starts on the control still scrolls the page, and inside a scrolling page the press waits a short content-touch delay. A commit the item's `onClick` vetoes sends the selection back at once; one a controlled parent adopts later lands without a bounce. With Reduce Motion nothing lifts, chases or travels: at the lift the selection cross-fades, and the pressed segment dims on the same 200 ms fade (`design:reduced-motion`). Mouse, keyboard and assistive technology keep the click; no element is added (the lifted shadow is the control's own pseudo-element).
