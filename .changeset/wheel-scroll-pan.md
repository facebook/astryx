[feat] On a touch device with a trackpad or a mouse, a two-finger scroll or a mouse wheel over a TimeInput, DateTimeInput or DateInput sheet's picker wheel now turns it the way a finger does: the wheel keeps the scroll's speed when it ends, coasts on and rests on a row. A ctrl-wheel, as a trackpad pinch arrives, zooms the page as before.
