[feat] Under a finger (a coarse primary pointer, on every touch device), a checkbox or radio mark now changes in the frame the selection lands, the way UIKit shows a selection mark. The box or circle used to fade between its two colors over the theme duration, so a tap first showed half-filled frames. With a mouse the marks keep the theme duration and easing, and under Reduce Motion a mouse's change is now immediate.
