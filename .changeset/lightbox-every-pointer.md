[feat] Lightbox: a mouse, a pen and a trackpad now use the same gestures as a finger. Drag a photo down to close the viewer; drag sideways, or scroll a trackpad sideways, to move through a gallery; ctrl-wheel to zoom, and out from the fitted size to close; drag or scroll to pan a zoomed photo, which coasts on when released moving. A double-click zooms in at the click, as a double tap does, and a zoom by a pointer hides the controls as a finger's does; the keyboard keeps its 2× zoom about the centre.
