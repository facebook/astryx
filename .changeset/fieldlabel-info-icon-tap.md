[feat] FieldLabel: a tap on the label's info icon (`labelTooltip`) opens its help without activating the control. On a touch screen, the tap that opened the help also focused the field, raising the keyboard, or toggled the switch or checkbox the label names. A tap by finger or pen now stops at the help; a mouse click on the icon still activates the control like the rest of the label, and hover still shows the help.
