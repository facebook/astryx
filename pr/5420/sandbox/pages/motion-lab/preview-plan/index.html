Rows where the brief and the audit disagree
8 verdicts do not survive the measurement
Almost all of them are the same mechanism: the brief credits a component with the motion it inherits, and the audit measures per directory. Tooltip, HoverCard, Popover and the overflow layers get their entry from Layer/; FieldStatus gets its slide from hooks/containerReveal.stylex.ts. Neither reading is wrong, but a rubric scoring components one at a time will score these wrong unless it knows where to look.
